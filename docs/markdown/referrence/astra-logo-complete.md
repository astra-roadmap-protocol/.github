## 🎯 THE 12 COMPLETE VARIANTS

### **FULL-COLOR VARIANTS (3)**

#### 1. PRIMARY MARK
- **What**: Main logo - star + path in teal
- **Use**: App icons, website headers, badges, main branding
- **Color**: Teal #208A91
- **Sizes**: 32px to 1024px
- **Formats**: SVG, PNG, PDF, EPS
- **Key Spec**: Geometric star with flowing path extension

#### 2. INVERTED (White/Light)
- **What**: White star + path for dark backgrounds
- **Use**: Dark mode, dark UI backgrounds, overlays, night theme
- **Color**: White #FFFFFF
- **Sizes**: 128px to 512px minimum
- **Contrast**: 10:1+ (WCAG AAA compliant)
- **Test Background**: Dark teal #0D2D35, navy #1A202C, black

#### 3. SECONDARY MARK
- **What**: Icon with optional "ASTRA" wordmark below
- **Use**: Social media profiles, newsletters, author bylines
- **Color**: Teal #208A91 + text
- **Gap**: 10px minimum between icon and text
- **Alignment**: Center aligned

---

### **SINGLE-COLOR VARIANTS (2)**

#### 4. SINGLE-COLOR BLACK
- **What**: Solid black star + path
- **Use**: Cost-effective printing, embroidery, screen print, stamping
- **Color**: Black #0F1419 (CMYK 0,0,0,100 for print)
- **No**: Gradients, transparency, variations
- **DPI**: 300 minimum for print quality

#### 5. SINGLE-COLOR WHITE
- **What**: Solid white star + path
- **Use**: White embroidery on dark apparel, white foil on dark materials
- **Color**: White #FFFFFF (opaque, not transparent)
- **Best For**: Dark fabric, dark packaging, dark backgrounds
- **Print Methods**: Screen print, foil stamping, embroidery

---

### **SPECIAL EFFECT VARIANT (1)**

#### 6. REVERSED-OUT
- **What**: Colored background with white negative space (logo cutout)
- **Use**: Bold signage, buttons, promotional materials, dramatic designs
- **Background**: Teal #208A91 (primary) or Orange #E68159 (accent)
- **Logo**: White or transparent negative space
- **Impact**: Bold, eye-catching, modern

---

### **TECHNICAL VARIANTS (4)**

#### 7. FAVICON SUITE
- **What**: Browser tab icons (multiple sizes)
- **Sizes**: 16×16, 32×32, 64×64, 128×128 px
- **Format**: favicon.ico (bundled) + individual PNG files
- **Critical**: Must be recognizable at 16px (main size)
- **Use**: Browser tabs, bookmarks, address bar, shortcuts
- **Color**: Teal #208A91

#### 8. APP ICON SUITE
- **What**: Icons optimized for app stores (iOS, Android, desktop)
- **iOS**: 120×120, 152×152, 180×180, 1024×1024 px
- **Android**: 192×192, 512×512 px
- **Desktop**: 128×128, 256×256, 512×512, 1024×1024 px
- **Safe Area**: 20% padding minimum
- **Use**: App store listings, home screen icons, app launchers

#### 9. SUBMARK (Minimal)
- **What**: Simplified version (path optional at very small sizes)
- **Versions**: 
  - With path (48px+) - full detail
  - Without path (24px+) - minimal, simple star
- **Use**: Small spaces, sidebar icons, tight layouts, badges
- **Advantage**: Maximum legibility in constrained spaces

#### 10. MONOGRAM (Letter "A" Alternative)
- **What**: Letter "A" incorporated into or surrounded by star
- **Options**:
  - "A" inside star circle (emblem style)
  - "A" formed by star points (geometric abstract)
  - "A" with star outline (traditional seal)
- **Use**: Badge, emblem, seal format, alternative branding
- **Format**: Square badge (120×120 px)

---

### **LOCKUP VARIANTS (2)**

#### 11. HORIZONTAL LOCKUP
- **What**: Logo on left + "ASTRA" text on right (side-by-side)
- **Use**: Website headers, social media bios, horizontal layouts
- **Format**: Rectangle (2-3:1 aspect ratio)
- **Gap**: 20-30px between logo and text
- **Alignment**: Vertically centered

#### 12. STACKED LOCKUP
- **What**: Logo above "ASTRA" text (stacked vertically)
- **Use**: Business cards, signage, vertical layouts, badges
- **Format**: Square to tall (1-1.4:1 aspect)
- **Gap**: 15-20px between logo and text
- **Alignment**: Center aligned
